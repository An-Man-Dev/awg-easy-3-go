Two-factor authentication

Replacing recovery codes invalidates the previous set. Disabling two-factor removes the authenticator and all recovery codes. Either action signs out your other sessions.

Recovery codes can replace your authenticator when signing in or changing security settings. If you lose all codes, ask your administrator to reset your second factor.

Connect an authenticator

Keep the setup key private. Your app should use 6 digits and a 30-second period.

Save your recovery codes

Shown once, for up to 5 minutes. Save these somewhere private before closing. Each code works once, together with your password.

Reset second factor?

This removes the account’s authenticator and recovery codes, and signs out all its sessions. Its password and VPN profiles stay the same. The owner must enroll an authenticator again.
